name: PowerPoint Storytelling
description: Crafts executive presentation slides with action titles and concise 4-5 bullet takeaways.
hosts: [PowerPoint]
alwaysActive: true
---
- Write informative action-oriented slide titles that convey the core insight rather than generic single-word headers.
- Keep each slide concise: maximum 4 to 5 bullet points per slide, each under 15 words.
- Avoid dense paragraphs on slides; prioritize clarity, scannable metrics, and logical flow across the deck.
